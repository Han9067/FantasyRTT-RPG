- The creator has personal details only at the top, and a portrait on the lower left with two columns of part selectors without scrolling (weapon selectors at upper right) on the right. Gender selects the matching head and hair for the current skin and hair color; eyes share its pose. Creator is human-only; hats/gloves/race have no selectors. Bare hands follow skin. Select primary type/item from melee except shields, bows and crossbows. Keep ranged types appended to stored type indices and use the separate display order. Select secondary type/item from one-handed melee including shields. Shields draw over the offhand. Other secondary weapons draw beneath its grip. Two-handed primary items clear and disable secondary equipment. Keep headgear/glove source assets for future equipment; mounts remain excluded.
- Compose 128x96 frames at integer coordinates; center the creator preview on the combined visible idle body and face bounds, with an 80x56 minimum crop expanded symmetrically to fit all poses. Keep body PNGs 01–08 per outfit. Idle/walk have 4 frames each; attack has 6 frames at 10fps, matching the weapon-specific chop_attack_sample.gif or thrust_attack_sample.gif with fixed body/head and no extra recovery pause. spear is thrust_attack; every other held weapon uses chop_attack. Preview controls are idle/walk/attack; respect per-frame durations. Head/accessory offsets follow the body. Use nearest filtering. World/battle feet are the unit position.
- Legacy assets/characters, assets/adventurers, tests/fixtures, and tools/references were explicitly removed by the user. Do not restore them.

Use art/world/tiled/WorldMap.tmx as the current editable terrain/city source; no procedural replacement layouts or old city names. Read docs/world/world-prototype.md for geography references and prototype limitations. The top-center calendar controls world time and travel speed. The battle button below the time display enters BattleMap; its return button restores world position/camera/zoom. Do not change battle mechanics while working on the campaign prototype. Battle uses a fixed 2560x1600 world, a bounded Camera2D with WASD pan and cursor-anchored wheel zoom 1x..3x, and a CanvasLayer HUD. Sprite scale stays 1; minimum camera zoom shows native pixels. Keep nearest filtering, spawn the hero ahead of the left infantry formation, click/four-direction box selection, selection-gated right-click walk orders and idle on arrival. All pointer positions must pass through the camera canvas transform; HUD hit areas remain in screen coordinates. UI screens restore canvas_items scaling. Terrain is a static procedurally baked texture with deeper green grass and no stone decorations; do not regenerate it on camera movement, zoom or window resizing.
